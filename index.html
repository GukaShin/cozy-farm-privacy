Cozy Farm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Summary

Cozy Farm does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The game works completely offline.

What the app stores

Your farm progress (level, coins, crops, animals, buildings and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No internet access: the app does not request the Internet permission.

Permissions

The only permission is vibration, used for gentle haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is collected either.

Changes

If a future version adds optional features such as cloud saves or ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.
