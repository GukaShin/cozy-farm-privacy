Cozy Farm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Summary

Cozy Farm does not collect or store any personal data on our own servers. Your farm is saved only on your device. The game offers optional rewarded videos provided by Google AdMob; only when you choose to watch one does the ad service process data, as described below.

What the app stores

Your farm progress (level, coins, crops, animals, buildings and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Optional rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video to get free gems or coins. Watching is never required to play. To show these videos, the app uses Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information (model, operating system, language);
  • information about the ads shown and how you interact with them;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), you are asked for consent before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services of our own.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No banner or pop-up ads: only videos you choose to watch.

Permissions

Vibration is used for gentle haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings). Internet and the advertising ID are used only for the optional rewarded videos. The game itself works offline.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are requested in a way that respects Google's policies for families where applicable.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page before the version of the app that needs it is released.
